Use Kết nối tri thức (KNTT) as the single textbook series for every subject and grade: lesson/unit order, knowledge map rows, RAG scope filters. Do not offer, design for or fill Cánh Diều or Chân trời sáng tạo.

**Why:** owner decided on one series so the knowledge map, lesson sets and exam scope have one stable order; per-edition rows were extra work with no use yet.
**How to apply:** every KnowledgeUnit carries `book.series = "Kết nối tri thức"`; a source for a row is the KNTT contents page or the programme text. If a school uses another series, that is a future decision for the owner, not a default. Related: [[mas-target-design-owner-decisions-2026-10-22]], [[matrix-is-one-instance]].
