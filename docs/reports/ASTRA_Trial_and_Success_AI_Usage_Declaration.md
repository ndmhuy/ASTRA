## 1. Disclosure of AI Assistance

The team acknowledges the use of generative artificial intelligence, including **ChatGPT (OpenAI)**, to assist in preparing and presenting the ASTRA project documentation. Such assistance included structuring and synthesizing supplied meeting notes and draft materials, refining English-language explanations, improving clarity and consistency, and preparing report content and document formatting. We used *Claude* for image rendering too.

## 2. Origin of the Project Work

The problem definition, computational-thinking discussions, alternative ideas, proposed system workflow, project decisions, and division of responsibilities described in the ASTRA report are based on the team's project materials and recorded discussions. AI assistance was used to organize and articulate these materials; it should not be interpreted as independent proof that a particular activity, implementation, or experiment occurred.

## 3. Verification and Academic Responsibility

The team retains responsibility for reviewing AI-assisted text against original records, evaluating proposed explanations and suggestions, correcting errors, and acknowledging external sources as appropriate. Generative AI is not considered a substitute for the team's own reasoning or for evidence supporting technical and academic claims.

The report distinguishes **documented activity**, **proposed or planned functionality**, and **performance targets**. Targets such as accuracy or formatting compliance are not claimed as achieved unless supported by actual evaluation results.

## 4. Declaration

We disclose the use of generative AI as a supporting tool in the preparation of the written ASTRA report. The submitted work is intended to represent the team's documented research, deliberations, and project decisions, subject to final review and approval by the team.
