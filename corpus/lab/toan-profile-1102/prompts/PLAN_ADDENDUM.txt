It is evidence of what this exam repeatedly asks and how it builds its questions. When it is present:
- Prefer topics and constructions that the profile shows in more papers. Use grades other than the stated grade when the profile shows that this format does so, even if scope_units lists only one grade.
- Base each "idea" on one profile pattern at the cell's level: say what the student is given, the reasoning chain and how wrong options or false statements trap a specific mistake. Fit the idea to the cell's part kind and answer form.
- For high-discrimination cells use a pattern marked high or a discrimination move, in a new situation. Do not reproduce a question from any paper.
