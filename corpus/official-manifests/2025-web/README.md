# Official 2025 THPT exam papers: Toán, Vật lí, Hóa học (collected 2026-10-23)

All six are the Ministry's "ĐỀ THI CHÍNH THỨC", Kỳ thi tốt nghiệp THPT năm 2025 (Bộ GD&ĐT), verified by looking at page 1 of each. Same questions across codes, different order. The full set is 24 codes per subject (Toán 0101-0124, Vật lí 0201-0224, Hóa học 0301-0324); only two codes per subject were downloaded.

QUALITY WARNING: none has a text layer. They are phone photos/scans of candidates' copies, with handwritten circles/marks and a partly redacted name block (the Ministry's own distribution was in this form). They need OCR or vision before anyone can label difficulty. hoa-hoc-2025-ma-0302.pdf has 3 pages while ma 0301 has 4, so it may be incomplete; use 0301 first.
