# Provincial and school Toán exams, 2025-2026 school year (thi thử tốt nghiệp THPT 2026)

Downloaded 2026-10-29 with the owner's approval, from toanmath.com (a document site; re-typeset copies, NOT the originals issued by the Sở GD&ĐT). One folder per paper: `paper.pdf` and `source.json` (page URL, file URL, SHA-256, pages, text-layer heuristic). `manifest.csv` lists all 75. Includes the official 2026 Ministry Toán paper (`de-chinh-thuc-...`), also a third-party copy.

Each PDF is usually the exam, an answer table and worked solutions together. Internal evidence only; the repo stays private and nothing here ships in the product.

Provenance warning: the six files in `../provincial-toan-unverified/` do NOT match the same-named Sở papers here on question 1 (checked for Hà Tĩnh, Bắc Ninh lần 2, Đà Nẵng on 2026-10-29), so treat them as app reconstructions or other rounds until proven otherwise.
