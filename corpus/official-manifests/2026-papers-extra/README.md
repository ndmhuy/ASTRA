# Real 2026 Ministry papers for Tiếng Nhật, Tiếng Hàn, Tiếng Nga (collected 2026-10-29, owner approved)

These are the exam papers of the national graduation exam 2026, copied via third-party hosts (the Ministry does not publish a stable download). Internal evidence only; the repo is private and nothing here ships in the product.

- tieng-nhat/: five mã đề (1630, 1628, 1627, 1625, 1603) as PDF scans, no text layer. Source: cdn.thuvienphapluat.vn/phap-luat/2022-2/PTT/13062026/<mã>_TN.pdf, linked from thuvienphapluat.vn "Đề TIẾNG NHẬT thi tốt nghiệp THPT 2026 CHÍNH THỨC" (page id 273917). Papers are 3 to 4 pages; the PDF for 1628 has 3 pages (check if one is missing).
- tieng-han/: mã đề 1725, pages 1 and 2 as photos (1080x1440) plus one more photo from a social post; the printed text says "Đề thi có 04 trang", so pages 3 and 4 are NOT here. Photos carry pencil marks and circled answers: read printed text only, never take an answer from a mark. Source: cdn.thuvienphapluat.vn/phap-luat/2022-2/BDHQ/120626/, page id 273936.
- tieng-nga/: mã đề 1225, four page images (about 600x850, low resolution) from sach-online.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/TN1..TN4.png. Low resolution: expect unreadable cells.
